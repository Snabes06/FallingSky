Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (6/10)

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Revenant Horror

Kill 10,000 Revenant Horror of tier II or higher.Use Ender Armor and a Void Sword to kill Crypt Ghouls in the Hub.Use your Bingo A?iphone to contact Maddox without travelling back to the Bar.

Fisherman

Wear 4 pieces of the Angler Armor Set.Purchase Angler Armor at the Fish Merchant. None.

Tropical Fish Collector

Reach 400 Tropical Fish Collection.Fish 50 Raw Cod, then purchase the rest from the Fish Merchant. Craft and place 10 Tier 1 Fishing Minions.This will take about 1 day.

Rock Solid

Equip a Common or higher tiered Rock Pet.Reach Heart of the Mountain 3, unlock Pickobulus, and mine Pure Gold in the Royal Mines.Mine Gold as there is a 60,000 Gold collection goal.

Fashionable

Wear 18 unique Armor Sets.Get the following armor sets:

Leather, Iron, Chainmail (Iron Forger), Gold, Diamond, Leaflet, Park Set (1.21.5), Farm Suit, Mushroom, Pumpkin, Cactus, Prospectors, Angler (8/8), Salmon (Fishing Minions), Rosetta, Squire, Celeste, Mercenary		Alternative sets include:
Ender Armor (8/8)
Lapis Armor (RNG)
Miner Armor (RNG)
Glacite Armor (RNG)
Goblin Armor (RNG)
Starlight Armor (180,000 coins)
Canopy Armor (500,000 coins, Galatea)
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Emerald Treasure

Unlock an Emerald Dungeon Chest.Get an Entrance and a Floor 1 carry with 230+ score. None.

Clown Collab

Defeat Bonzo 5,000 times.Use Party Finder or get carried.With Catacombs Level 9, some Rotton Armor, a Hyper Cleaver and Dungeon Potions, you can solo clear Bonzo with the Berserker class on Bingo with moderate ease.

Gold Ingot Collector

Reach 60,000 Gold Ingot Collection.Reach Heart of the Mountain 3, unlock Pickobulus, and mine Pure Gold in the Dwarven Mines.Don't forget to upgrade your Mining Fortune Perk.

Slayer

Reach Level 3 for any Slayer boss.Kill Revenant Horror bosses with Crypt Ghouls until you reach Revenant Slayer 3.None.

Sponge Collector

Reach 900 Sponge Collection.Fish 50 Raw Cod, then purchase the rest from the Fish Merchant.
Craft and place 10 Tier 1 Fishing Minions.		This will take 2-3 days
Row #3​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Powder Collector

Collect 50,000 Mithril Powder.Forge Mithril Equipment in the Dwarven Forge, then complete Mining Events.None.

New Savings

Craft a Piggy Bank.Craft and place 2 Tier 1 Pig Minions with Medium Storages.This will take about 2 days.

Lucky Looter

Loot 250k drops with a chance of 1% or lowerKill Endermen in the End.You can either use a Heroic Dreadlord Sword with Wise Dark/Shadow Goggles and Ender Armor, or a Void Sword with Fierce Ender Armor.

Make sure you have at least Luck 5 and Looting 3 on your weapon.

You can get Luck 6 and Looting 4 from Einary during Jerry's Workshop.

Strongman

Reach 160 ? Strength.Hold a Void Sword while wearing 2 pieces of Ender Armor.None.

Lumberjack

Obtain level 5 in the Foraging Skill.Chop Oak Trees in the Hub.None.
Row #4​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Critical

Reach 100% ? Crit Damage.Wear 4 pieces of Fierce Ender Armor in the End.None.

Exterminator

Kill 10,000 mobs.Kill Endermen in your Private Island following this tutorial.Alternatively, kill Enderman on the End Island to progress in Lucky Looter and Scavenger.

Collecting Data

Reach Bestiary Milestone 5.Passive.Make sure to talk to Bramass Beastslayer.

Skilled

Gain 750M Mining experienceComplete Dwarven Commissions in the Dwarven Mines.Make sure to use a Silverfish Pet, the Prospector's reforge (until you can reforge it with Refined at Mining 25) and a Level 100 Bingo Pet to maximise rates.

Sea Summoner

Reach 30% a Sea Creature Chance.Equip 8/8 Angler Armor, and enchant a fishing rod with Angler 5.None.
Row #5​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Experienced

Obtain a Grand Experience Bottle.Mine End Nodes in the End.Alternatively, you can do experiments, or mine Lapis Lazuli in the Dwarven Mines to craft one.

Esteemed Enchanter

Obtain a Level VI Enchanted Book.Purchase this from the Floor 1 to get a Level 6 book. However, if you do not get a Level 6 book from there, purchase a Stonk from the End Island for 499,999 coins.None.

Healthy

Reach 250 ? Health.Passive.None.

King Of The... Seas?

Fish up a Carrot KingUnlock Fishing 14 by fishing in the Backwater Bayou and collecting from minions.

Craft Salmon Armor and Carrot Bait using the fish from your Fishing Minions.		You can get Frog Pet from Galatea to improve your Fishing Speed.
You can also give Nope the Fish to Carol by beating the End Race in under 80 seconds then visiting her in Galatea.

Scavenger

Loot 250M Coins through the Scavenger EnchantmentKill Endermen in the End.Make sure to have Scavenger 3 on your Sword. Scavenger 4 can be purchased from Einary during Jerry's Workshop


Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (6/10)
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a shovel.
Enchant the shovel with Efficiency and dig sand in the Mushroom Desert until Mining 12
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines
Reach Coal 3 (250 coal collection)
Purchase 640 Coal from the Mine Merchant
Purchase 640 Oak Logs from the Lumber Merchant
Chop 115 Oak Logs, then use the /trades menu to convert the 755 Oak Logs to 1510 Coal, for a total of 2400 coal.
Craft 15 Enchanted Coal, and give to Rhys to unlock Heart of the Mountain

Recommended Minions​


Recommended Minion Slots: 10​

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000)
Mine sand to reach mining XII for the Dwarven Mines.
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!

Guide by Siggydlx and ZerosTulip (Tanya the Evil)
