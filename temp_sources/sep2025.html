Most Useful

Bingo Brewers Discord (Free God Splashes)
The Bingo Brewers mod has released in Beta with ingame splash notifications and Crystal Hollows chest waypoints.
Derpy is mayor from day 2 to day 6. You will need to have minions running during this time for his x2 minion output perk.


Overall Bingo Difficulty: Hard (7/10)

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

KABOOM!

Blow up 75k Crypts in The Catacombs.Buy Superboom TNTs from Ophelia in the Dungeon Hub, or Walter in the Deep Caverns.Walter is 500 coins cheaper than Ophelia, and it adds up fast.

Search And Rescue

Help Fetchur find his item.Self-ExplanatoryDay 1: 20x Yellow Stained Glass
Day 2: 1x Compass
Day 3: 20x Mithril
Day 4: 1x Firework Rocket
Day 5: 1x Cheap/Decent/Black Coffee
Day 6: 1x Wooden Door
Day 7: 3x Rabbit Foot

Exterminator

Kill 10,000 mobs.Kill Enderman on your Private Island (TUTORIAL).Read the description for dimensions. Credit to Yarrs and oslick
If you get New Savings (Craft a Piggy Bank) first, you'll make good progress in this goal.

Pair Up!

Find 1 Pair in the Experimentation Table.Reach Enchanting 10, then craft an Experimentation Table.None

Clay Collector

Reach 260,000 Clay Collection.Unlock Backwater Bayou, then kill Backwater Sea Creatures until you reach 50 clay.
Reach Seed Collection 2 and trade Seeds for clay in the /trades menu.
Craft at least 5 T1 clay minions.

Each minion will make 5400 a day, totalling 27000. During Derpy, this is increased to 54000, and will take 5 days to complete.		Make sure to have Medium Storage, upgrade your minions whenever you can, and collect frequently.

You are strongly advised to use Compactors to make the minions fill slower.
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Extra Storage

Craft a Large Storage.Purchase Canopy Armor from Amaury in Galatea, then gather Enchanted Jungle Logs to craft a Treecapitor, and finally head back to the Hub and chop 42k Oak Logs.Use Mega hubs as the logs replenish faster.

A Hard Day's Work

Collect 100k Jacob's Tickets from Farming Events!Unlock Garden, and plant wheat, carrot and potato plots for easy bronze medals.Use the calender (/events) to see what crops are coming next, and cycle plot presets accordingly.

Wheat, Carrot and Potato are all easy for Bronze. Cocoa Beans are good for Silver. Cactus and Mushroom are good for Gold.

Mutton Collector

Reach 50,000 Mutton Collection.Craft at least 3 T1 Sheep Minions.

Each minion will make 1800 a day, totaling 5400. During Derpy, this is increased to 10800, and will take just under 5 days to complete.		Make sure to have Medium Storage, upgrade your minions whenever you can, and collect frequently.

Sheep Minions fill much faster than both Clay Minions (with Compactors) and Creeper Minions due to their wool output!

Slaughterer

Kill any tier II Slayer Boss within 80 seconds.Obtain 5/8 Ender Armor and a Void Sword, then progress through Revenant Slayer.Your Bingo A?iphone will have Maddox's Contact already added.

Gunpowder Collector

Reach 45,000 Gunpowder Collection.Kill Sneaky Creepers in the Deep Caverns until you unlock the Creeper Minion recipe. Craft at least 3 T1 Creeper Minions.

Each minion will make 1.6k a day, totalling 4800. During Derpy, this is increased to 9600, and will take just under 5 days to complete.		Alternatively, you can build a large room on your Private Island for creepers to spawn in.
Make sure to have Medium Storage, upgrade your minions whenever you can, and collect frequently.

Description​
Method​
Notes​

Bedazzled

Equip 22 unique Accessories.Collection Talismans [9]:
Farming Talisman, Speed Talisman, Potion Affinity Talisman, Feather Talisman, Talisman of Coins, Magnetic Talisman, Wood Affinity Talisman, Piggy Bank, Gravity Talisman.

Shop Talismans [6]
Scavenger Talisman, Intimidation Talisman, Village Affinity Talisman, Mine Affinity Talisman, Zombie Talisman, Skeleton Talisman.

Miscellaneous [7]
Archaeologist's Compass, Campfire Badge, Soul Campfire Badge, Spider Talisman, Mediocre Ring of Love, Wolf's Paw
Pig's Foot		If you have Bingo Rank 3 and 4, your Personal Compactor/Deletor will count to this goal.
Relevant alternatives: If you're doing the community goal 'A Hard Day's Work', then Pesthunter, Anita and Night Vision Talisman are all viable.

New Savings

Craft a Piggy Bank.Acquire a Rare Chicken Pet from Kat and kill Pigs in the Barn.Alternatively, unlock an extra minion slot and place a single T1 Pig Minion with a Medium Storage.

Pet

Gain 2.5B Pet experience.Use Carrot Candies on Silverfish pets.Mining Commissions and Foraging in Galatea are also viable. Make sure to use Pet EXP Boosters purchased from Zog.

Hyper Reforger

Apply the Hyper reforge to an item.Mine Ender Nodes for an End Stone Geode, then apply it to armor.You will also need a Grand Experience Bottle from the nodes for the Experienced goal.

Podium Position

Obtain a Bronze or higher Medal in Jacob's Farming Contest.Farm Wheat in the Hub during a Jacob's Contest.You will need to get Farming 10 and talk to Jacob to participate in contests.
If you are struggling, unlock Garden to farm on a plot.

Description​
Method​
Notes​

Trouble is Brewing...

Brew any level IV or higher potion.Brew a Mana 4 potion with Enchanted Mutton, Awkward Potions and Glowstone Dust.You can obtain Glowstone Dust by purchasing Glowstone from the Builder and breaking it on your private island.

I See Stars

Apply 5 Stars to a dungeon itemTalk to the Gold Forger at the Gold Mine, then complete a trade when he visits on your Garden, then upgrade Super Cleaver (purchased from Ophelia) at Malik for 9 Gold Essence. This will take a couple visits.Alternatively, you can complete Marigold's questline to get the Gold Essence.

Sightseer

Visit 7 different public islands.Self-ExplanatoryNone

Operation: Ore

Break 10M Ore Blocks.Mine Pure Ores in the Royal Mines or the Mines of Divan.Dwarven Metals do not count as contribution to this goal. This includes Mithril and Titanium. Use /blockguide and navigate to "Ores" to see all viable options.

Love Is All Around

Obtain a Mediocre Ring of Love.Follow the Romero Questline to get this.You only need 1 Yellow Rock, 15 Poppies, and 64 Emeralds.
Row #5​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Experienced

Obtain a Grand Experience Bottle.Mine Ender Nodes until you drop a Grand Experience Bottle.You also need an End Stone Geode, so focus on end stone nodes.

Pet Hoarder

Obtain 6 unique Pets.Bingo, Grandma Wolf, Silverfish, Jerry, Chicken, ZombieYou can now kill Chickens to drop eggs.
If you get 2,500 Chicken Collection, you can unlock Enchanted Eggs and craft Ocelot to help with Lumberjack.

Eagle-Eyed

Complete a timed Target Practice.Self-ExplanatoryUse an Artisanal Shortbow purchased from the Weaponsmith.

Lumberjack

Obtain level 20 in the Foraging Skill.Craft a Treecapitor in the Park, purchase Canopy Armor from Amaury then chop Oak Logs for Large Storage.You can also get the enchanted egg for Ocelot by unlocking Chicken Collection 5 while doing New Savings. Once you finish getting your Large Storage, and all the Medium Storages for your minions, chopping down Fig Trees after talking to Agatha in Murkwater Loch is the fastest way to get Foraging EXP

Essence Of Spiders

Collect 1M Spider Essence.Kill Arachne, and salvage Arachnal Armor.Higher level bosses will drop more Essence.


Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Hard (7/10)
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a sword.
Go to the Spider's Den and get the 3 easy relics (-142, 82, -340/-201, 83, -352/-341, 122, -252).
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines.

Recommended Minions​

Recommended Minion Slots: 11+ ​

Strongly Recommended
You will need 5 T1 Clay Minions up by day 2 of Bingo. Upgrade them at any opportunity you can. Compactors, Medium Storage and Enchanted Charcoal are recommended.
You will need 3 T1 Creeper Minions up by day 2 of Bingo. Upgrade them at any opportunity you can. Medium Storage and Enchanted Charcoal are recommended.
You will need 3 T1 Sheep Minions up by day 2 of Bingo. Upgrade them at any oppurtunity you can. Medium Storage and Enchanted Charcoal are recommended. Sheep Minion fill up extremely fast due to generating wool as well as mutton. Make sure to collect frequently.
Optional
You may want to run a Pig Minion for the Piggy Bank. If you do this, make sure to get an extra minion slot so you do not interfere with the required minions. Don't upgrade it, keep the Porkchops for the Piggy Bank.

Remember, days 2 to 6 are buffed by Derpy's "TURBO MINIONS!!!" perk.
Clay Minion
Five T1 Clay = 27000 a day.
x2 (Derpy) for day 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 = 270k Clay.

Creeper Minion
Three T1 Creeper = 4800 a day.
x2 (Derpy) for days 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, = 48k Gunpowder.

Sheep Minion
Three T1 Sheep Minion = 5400 a day.
x2 (Derpy) for days 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 = 54k Mutton.

Pig Minion
One T1 Pig Minion = 1662 a day.
Any 4 days = 6.4k Porkchop.

Enchanted Charcoal increases all of these by 20%.

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Purchase All EXP Boost from Zog and level up your Bingo Pet as much as you can for it's Lucky Looter perk.
Spend downtime unlocking more minion slots, fuel, and/or farming Sheep at the Shepherd's Keep.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000)
Derpy is present from 8:25am BST Day 2, till 12:25pm BST day 6. You'll want your clay minions down during this time, as they'll fill the slowest with Compactors.
Mine sand to reach mining XII for the Dwarven Mines.
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!

I wanted to show what is really faster, placing minions one after another or at the same time, so i calculated it with gunpowder and clay as example. you can see the calculation in the pdf. unfortunately i made a mistake.

I assumed that a clay minion produces 1 clay at a time, but it actually produces 4. at first I thought this is not a problem, because in the calculation where both minions are placed at the same time, I also assumed 1 clay is produced. this means the total time is not exact, but you could still see what is faster since both start with the same condition.

the problem I noticed later is that in the simultaneous setup there will be a point when one goal is done, and the remaining minions would switch to clay, because more is needed. i cannot track this correctly if i assume only 1 clay instead of 4, so i paused the calculation for now.

but if anyone is interested, I can recalculate everything to get a final result. I thought it’s not worth starting over if nobody needs it. everything I have done so far is available in the pdf if you want to check it out (this only includes placing all of one type, placing them at the same time is not included yet)
