Bingo Brewers Discord (Free God Splashes)

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Extreme

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Dilmun Trader

Collect 1.25M Copper from Garden Visitors.Paste single plot Potato, Carrot, Wheat, and Sugar Cane farms to easily supply the most common required crops.You will need to either deny any visitors asking for other crops, or build out farms for other crops as you unlock them.

Yummers

Open 15 Cans of Worms.Fish for and kill or lootshare Trash Gobblers in the Backwater Bayou.Use a Junk Sinker so you can start collecting Rusty Coins.
Make sure you have Looting 3 and increase your SCC.

Exterminator

Vacuum 100 Pests.Use the free Vacuum you get and farm the crops you need for visitors. Make sure you purchase available pest upgrades.None

Spider Squisher

Kill 75 spiders in 90 seconds. (Spiders on your private island do not count)Kill Spiders in the Arachne's Burrow with a Dreadlord Sword and mage gear, or a strong cleave weapon. Focus on killing large groups at once.

The Mirror Dimension

Reach 1,000s ? Rift Time.Follow Rift Guide, prioritized the easiest available time such as Enigma Souls.None
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

End All, Be Al

Defeat a Zealot Bruiser.Reach Combat 20 by killing Endermen. Killing a Zealot Bruiser will require a bit of caution but is not difficult.None

Deep Sea Angler

Fish up 500k Sea Creatures of Rare or higher rarity.Fish in the Backwater Bayou for Banshee (Fishing Level 10), or other Rare Sea Creatures like Catfish.None

Old School

Craft a Runaan's Bow.Place Skeleton and Spider Minions to unlock and craft the bow.You can actively grind Bones using Gravel Skeletons in the Spider's Den.
You can actively grind Enchanted String from Arachne.

Pair Up!

Find 20 Pairs in the Experimentation Table.Make sure you consistently complete your daily experiments.None

Shh...

Find 100 Secrets in Dungeons.Utilize a Grappling Hook and Ender Pearls to navigate rooms to find secrets.
Do this on Floor 1 once you can solo it with 270 score.		None
Row #3​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Collector

Get 50 Collections to at least tier 2.This goal should be mostly passive, you may need to target a few collections after completing most of the card.None

Lighter than steel

Wear 4 pieces of the Mithril Equipment Set.Obtain 12 Enchanted Mithril and forge Mithril Equipment in the Forge.You can get 10 Enchanted Mithril from Bednom if you aren't able to craft it when necessary.

Skilled

Gain 2.5B Combat experienceKill Endermen in The End.None

Oh Captain, My Captain

Upgrade 2 Ship Parts to Bronze in your Ship.Fish in the Backwater Bayou and make a Bronze Ship Helm and Bronze Ship HullThis goal should be passive while completing other fishing goals.

Legal Duplication

Reach 270 ? Mining Fortune.You will need Mining Skill level 20 (+80), Mithril Equipment (+25), HOTM Mining Fortune level 20 (+40), Spelunker Potion (+20), Century Cake (+5), Better Together (+50).This list is only 220 Mining Fortune, hopefully Bingonimbus is available for +100 Mining Fortune, otherwise using a Fruitful Mithril Drill SX-R226 enchanted with Fortune 3 will get you an extra 61 and you will need a few levels.
Row #4​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Forgery

Forge a DrillForge a Mithril Drill SX-R226.
3 Refined Mithril
- Make sure you save Mithril you mine, you can also use Mithril Minions to make up part or all of the 480 Enchanted Mithril.
1 Fuel Canister
- Mine for 2 Enchanted Coal Blocks in the Royal Mines.
1 Drill Motor
- Kill Team Treasurite members in the Mithril Deposits for 10 Treasurite.
- Mine for an Enchanted Iron and 3 Enchanted Redstone Blocks in the Royal mines.
- Golden Plate
  • Mine for 2 Enchanted Diamond and Gold Blocks in the Royal Mines.
  • Kill Glacite Walkers for 5 Glacite Jewels.
This requires several days of forge time.
The required 480 Enchanted Mithril can be done with Mithril Minions.

Fisherman's Folly

Fish up an OUTSTANDING treasure or junk.The only way to grind this is by fishing normally, do so in a way that progresses other fishing related goals.None

Chocolatier

Collect a lifetime total of 25M Chocolate in the Chocolate Factory.Self-ExplanatoryThis will take active upgrading, but 25M Chocolate should not take more than a few days.

Commissioned

Complete 250k mining commissions.Complete commissions in the Dwarven Mines.The Dwarven Mines is better in most cases because it contributes towards the required 480 Enchanted Mithril to make a drill. Crystal Hollows commissions will be faster if you progress enough.

At your service

Complete 50 Garden Visitors' OffersPaste single plot Potato, Carrot, Wheat, and Sugar Cane farms to easily supply the most common required crops.Don't waste time on visitors that are too expensive unless you need the copper for Dilmun Trader.
Row #5​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Who needs friends?

Obtain a score of at least 270 in Catacombs Floor I without a partyUse a Super Cleaver with 4 pieces of Dungeon Armor and play as a Berserker.Use Mage's Thunderstorm to grind dungeon gear drops early on if you can't kill mobs.
If you are still struggling, you may need to increase your catacombs level. This can be done best with party finder or solo completion runs.

Bayou Bobbin'

Wear 4 pieces of the Backwater Armor Set.Use a Junk Sinker to fish up Rusty Coins for the armor.
Purchase Backwater Equipment.		Fish using Angler Armor since you need Sea Creatures for other goals.

Bedazzled

Equip 30 unique Accessories.Collection Talismans [10]:
Farming Talisman, Vaccine Talisman, Speed Talisman, Potion Affinity Talisman, Talisman of Coins, Magnetic Talisman, Gravity Talisman, Night Vision Charm, Wood Affinity Talisman, Emerald Ring.

Shop Talismans [6]:
Zombie Talisman, Skeleton Talisman, Village Affinity Talisman, Mine Affinity Talisman, Intimidation Talisman, Scavenger Talisman.

Miscellaneous [8]:
Wolf Paw (Click), Ring of Love, Archaeologist's Compass, Campfire Initiate Badge, Soul Campfire Initiate Badge Cat Talisman, Runeblade Talisman, Moonglade Talisman.
If Applicable: Personal Compactor and Deletor from Bingo Ranks

Pick 6 more talismans of the following:
Event/Time Gated Talismans:
Candy Talisman, Frozen Chicken, Glacial Talisman.

Harder/Out of the Way Collection Talismans:
Feather Talisman, Talisman of Power, Book of Progression, Sea Creature Talisman, Healing Talisman, Small Fish Bowl.

Other talismans:
Red Claw Talisman, Melody's Hair, King Talisman, Anita's Talisman, Pig's Foot		Pig's Foot is harder without grappling hook. Pig's Foot Route if you're struggling (Click).
For the Night Vision Charm use a silk touch axe on mushroom blocks to unlock the recipe.

They're Prrrrrretty good

Consume 4 boxes of Dwarven CerealParticipate in active Dwarven Mines events for Dwarven O's Ore Oats.None

Scavenger

Loot 750M Coins through the Scavenger EnchantmentEnsure your sword has scavenger, or go for a Dreadlord Sword with Scavenger 5.None


Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Extreme
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​

Recommendation: If you are looking for a more casual Bingo experience, I would recommend waiting a day or two before grinding anything beyond easy goals or basic minions and skills because we do not know what the special bingo items are yet or when they are obtained.

Recommended Minions​


Recommended Minion Slots: 5-10​

Minions and collections are not central to this bingo card.

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000)
Mine sand to reach mining XII for the Dwarven Mines.
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!

Guide by Indigo_Polecat and contributors in the Bingo Brewers discord.
