Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (7/10)

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Tarantula Broodfather

Kill 10,000 Tarantula Broodfather of tier II or higher.Use Ender Armor and Void Sword. You can use a Dreadlord Sword to speed up the spawning process. Ensure that your sword has Cleave. Utilise the Maddox contact in your Bingo Abiphone.

Collecting Data

Reach Bestiary Milestone 3.Passive.Ensure that you talk to Bramass Beastslayer. Talk to Grandmother wolf as well to get the Grandmother Wolf pet.

Healthy

Reach 1,000 ? Health.Equip Ender Armour and have a splash activeNone.

Bedazzled

Equip 7 unique Accessories.Buy 6 accessories from the Adventurer NPC in the Hub, and craft the Potion Affinity Talisman from the Netherwart collection. Personal Compactor or Personal Deletor can also be used for this goal.

Collector

Get 10 Collections to at least tier 7.It is recommended to get Diamond, Sand, Pork, Prismarine Crystals, Gold, Wheat, Seeds, Endstone, Gravel, and Redstone. Feel free to substitute the last three collections with any of the foraging collections.
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Powder Collector

Collect 25,000 Mithril Powder.Participate in active mining events in the Dwarven Mines. Claim your daily Fetchur and Puzzler while the 2x powder event is active.None.

Enderman Slayer

Kill 2.5M Endermen.Use Ender Armor and a Void Sword. You can also use a Dreadlord Sword to increase your rates. None.

Diamond Collector

Reach 40,000 Diamond Collection.Mine diamonds manually using Pure Ores that is located in the Royal Mines, Dwarven Mines. Use Prospecting Armour and a Bandaged Mithril pickaxe to do this. Ensure that you use your Pickobulus ability.It is recommended to upgrade Mining Speed and Mining Fortune, and get the Pickobulus ability.

Pet Hoarder

Obtain 3 unique Pets.Bingo pet, Grandma Wolf pet, Bee pet. Bee pet requires 4999 coins, 2 blocks of coal and gold.

Amber Crystal

Obtain an Amber Crystal in the Crystal Hollows.Obtain 1 Goblin Egg. Locate the King and the Queen and find the route between them. Visit the King and get the King's Scent, and get the Amber Crystal from the Queen.The King's Scent washes off in water.
Row #3​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Fashionable

Wear 15 unique Armor Sets.Leather, Iron, Chainmail, Gold, Diamond, Leaflet, Park set, Farm Suit, Mushroom, Pumpkin, Cactus, Angler, Rosetta's, Celeste, Squire. Chainmail is sold from the Iron Forger. Rosetta's Armor and Diamond Armor count as unique sets.

Carry-On

Craft a Small Backpack.Kill cows in the Barn until you have reach Leather 3Use an Artisanal Shortbow to speed up this process.

Poseidon

Summon 100k Sea Creatures.Use Salmon Armor and the Frog pet with a Glowing Chum Bucket active. Only your own Sea Creatures count towards this goal.

Friend Of The Seas

Equip an Uncommon or higher tiered Dolphin Pet.Fish 250 Sea Creatures using the Angler Set and Frog pet to get the common Dolphin pet. Upgrade the common Dolphin using Kat. Upgrading via Kat takes 24 hours. Make sure to fish in the Spiders Den while it rains. You could alternatively use Salmon Armor as you have fishing minions placed down.

Strongman

Reach 120 ? Strength.Passive.None.
Row #4​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

New Savings

Craft a Piggy Bank.Craft and place 2 Tier 1 Pig Minions with Medium Storages.This will take around 2 days.

Brewer

Obtain level 16 in the Alchemy Skill.Mine Gold through the Pure Ores in the Royal Mines, Dwarven Mines to craft enchanted gold. Brew enchanted gold into Absorption potions. You need about 50 to 60 enchanted gold to reach Alchemy 16.

A Lost Cause

Kill a Lost Adventurer.Play as a Mage, locate a Lost Adventurer and use your Ultimate ability. The Ultimate ability will continue to be in effect after death, and will kill the Lost Adventurer.None.

Bait

Deposit Chum in Chum Buckets.Use Salmon Armor and the Frog pet to get Chum. None.

Slayer

Reach Level 3 for any Slayer boss.Slay Revenant Horror bosses in the Hub until you reach Revenant Horror 3. Use the Ender Armor and Void Sword. Use the Bingo Abiphone that you are given.
Row #5​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Experienced

Obtain a Grand Experience Bottle.Mine End Nodes in the End. You could get lucky and get this through your experiments.None.

Sand Collector

Reach 47,500 Sand Collection.Use Prospecting Armor and an Enchanted Golden Shovel. It is recommended to do this once all the other mining goals are done, and you invest all your Mithril power into Mining Fortune.

Commission Master

Complete 4 Commissions in the Dwarven Mines, Crystal Hollows, or Glacite Tunnels.Self-explanatory. First 4 commissions are always mine 50 Mithril, mine 2 titanium, participate in your first event, and slay 50 creatures in the Mines.

Prismarine Crystals Collector

Reach 1,500 Prismarine Crystals Collection.Craft and place 8 Tier 1 Fishing Minions with Medium Storages. Get the fish from the Fishing Merchant NPC found in the Hub.This will take about 5 to 6 days.

Skilled

Gain 750M Mining experienceUse Prospecting Armour and a Bandaged Mithril Pickaxe to complete commissions from the King for Mining exp. None.


Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (7/10)
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a sword.
Go to the Spider's Den and get the 3 easy relics (-142, 82, -340/-201, 83, -352/-341, 122, -252).
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines.
Reach Coal 3 (250 coal collection)
Purchase 640 Coal from the Mine Merchant
Purchase 640 Oak Logs from the Lumber Merchant
Chop 115 Oak Logs, then use the /trades menu to convert the 755 Oak Logs to 1510 Coal, for a total of 2400 coal.
Craft 15 Enchanted Coal, and give to Rhys to unlock Heart of the Mountain

Recommended Minions​


Recommended Minion Slots: 10​

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000)
Mine sand to reach mining XII for the Dwarven Mines.
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!
