Most Useful

Bingo Brewers Discord (Free God Splashes)


Overall Bingo Difficulty: Easy (2/10)

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Lucky Looter

Loot 250k drops with a chance of 1% or lower.Kill Endermen in the End.You can either use a Heroic Dreadlord Sword with Wise Dark/Shadow Goggles and Ender Armor, or a Void Sword with Fierce Ender Armor.

Make sure you have at least Luck 5 and Looting 3 on your weapon.

You can get Luck 6 and Looting 4 from Einary during Jerry's Workshop.

Podium Position

Obtain a Bronze or higher Medal in Jacob's Farming Contest.Reach Farming 10, then harvest wheat during a Jacob's Contest in the hub.Use a Harvesting 5 hoe. You shouldn't need Garden for this.

Strongman

Reach 180 ? Strength.Hold a Void Sword while wearing at least 3/4 Ender Armor.None.

Cat

Obtain a Cat Talisman.Use this strategy to quicky unlock the Cat talisman.None.

Minion Machine

Obtain 100 unique Minions.Mining Minions [54]
Dwarven Mines [34]: Mithril (6), Coal (5), Iron (5), Gold (5), Redstone (4), Lapis (3), Emerald (3), Diamond (3)
Other [19]: End Stone (9), Sand (6), Cobblestone (4)
Combat Minions [15]
Spider (4), Cave Spider (4), Enderman (4), Zombie (3)
Foraging Minions [24]
Oak (4), Birch (4), Spruce (4), Dark Oak (4), Acacia (4), Jungle (4)
Farming Minions [7]
Wheat (4), Chicken (1), Pig (1), Cow (1)
There's no reason to go for Farming Minions (other than the ones listed), as you need Rock Pet.
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Sea Summoner

Reach 30% a Sea Creature Chance.Purchase 8/8 Angler Armor from the Fishing Merchant, and then enchant and hold an Angler 3 Fishing Rod.You should probably get Angler 5 so you have the best early-game Sea Creature Chance for the Skilled (Fishing) Community Goal.

Enderman Slayer

Kill 2.5M Endermen.Kill Endermen in the End.Alternatively you can use this strategy on your Private Island by building a killing chamber enclosed in dark and setting your island's biome to End by giving Jerry the End Biome Stick.

This is faster, but it won't get you Lucky Looter or Essence of Dragon contribution.

Relic Researcher

Find 28 Relics in the Spider's Den.SBE and SkyTils have waypoints for these.You can also find coordinates on wiki.

Slaughterer

Kill any tier II Slayer Boss within 80 seconds.Kill a T2 Revenant Horror.Do this after you get a Void or Dreadlord sword.

Battle-Ready

Wear 4 pieces of Equipment.Equip 4/4 Angler Equipment from the Fish Merchant.None.
Row #3​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

End Stone Collector

Reach 60,000 End Stone Collection.Mine Ender Nodes in the End, and kill the Nest Endermites for Enchanted End Stone.Alternatively, you can use 10 End Stone Minions (without fuel) to get this in 4 days.

Collecting Data

Reach Bestiary Milestone 5.Passive.If you must, use Mage Ultimate in dungeons to complete this.

Skilled

Gain 250M Fishing experienceFish in the Backwater Bayou with 8/8 Angler Armor, Lure 3 and Angler 5 on your fishing rod.Verdant Attribute Shard boosts fishing speed in Galatea by up to 30, and Bogged Attribute Shard increases Sea Creature Chance by up to 5.

Rock Solid

Equip a Rare or higher tiered Rock Pet.Do Mithril Commissions until you unlock Pickobulus. Then mine ores on one of the minecarts in the Dwarven Mines.
Give your Common Rock Pet to Kat to upgrade it to Rare, for 1.1m coins total. This will take 3 days to complete.		Mithril does work for Rock Pet. Cobblestone does not. This was tested 29/10/2025.

I Am Speed

Reach 200 ? Speed.Attend a Bingo Brewers Splash.Alternatively, use the Rogue Sword from Jamie to get this.
Row #4​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Emerald Treasure

Unlock an Emerald Dungeon Chest.Get carried by someone or use party finder.You need 230 score for the Emerald Chest.

Skill Average

Reach a non-cosmetic Skill Average of 14.
Self-Explanatory
Foraging in Galatea gives really good EXP yields. Craft a Treecap, purchase Canopy Armor from Amuary, then chop fig trees. Make sure you've spoke to Agatha so you get bonus EXP and tickets for Frog Pet.
You can easily reach Fishing 10 by completing the End Race in under 80 seconds to obtain Nope the Fish, and then giving it to Carol in Galatea for 10,000 fishing EXP.
Brew Absorption potions with Enchanted Gold Ingots to level Alchemy.

Pair Up!

Find 3 Pairs in the Experimentation Table.Reach Enchanting 10 and craft an Experimentation Table.None.

Essence Of Spiders

Collect 1M Spider Essence.Kill Arachne bosses.Higher level bosses will drop more Essence.
Remember to salvage Arachne gear!

Love Is All Around

Obtain a Mediocre Ring of Love.Do the Romero questline until you complete this.You need 1 Yellow Rock, 15 poppies, and 64 emeralds.
Row #5​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Taking Flight

Consume a Magical Mushroom Soup.Use a Silk Touch Axe to mine 1,000 Mushroom Blocks in the Mushroom Desert.

Once you reach Mushroom 4, craft a Bowl, get some Redstone and Glowstone Dust, then craft your Magical Mushroom Soup.		Silk Touch is still better at low fortune despite being nerfed.

To get Glowstone Dust, buy a Glowstone block from the Builder, and then break it on your island.

Weird Flex

Gain 5 ? Strength from a Training Weight.Get a Training Weight from dungeons and wait 100 minutes with it in your inventory.You should try to do this as early as you can.

Bedazzled

Equip 7 unique Accessories.Buy 6 accessories from the Adventurer, and talk to the Archaeologist in the Spiders' Den for the Archaeologist's Compass.Archaeologist's Compass, Talisman/Ring of Love and Cat Talisman are both required for this bingo, so you can save 20,000 coins if you know you're going to get them.

Pet Hoarder

Obtain 12 unique Pets.Craftable Pets (6)
Jerry, Pig, Chicken, Silverfish, Zombie, Horse
Non-Craftable Pets (6)
Bingo, Grandma Wolf, Bee, Enderman, Rock, Frog
You can replace any of these with Owl Pet (2.2m) or Reindeer (Jerry's Workshop).

If you're going for Fishing EXP, Lava Sea Creatures DO NOT count to Dolphin Milestone.

Frog Pet requires unlocking Agatha's Contests, which requires 1.21.5 and Galatea.

Essence Of Dragons

Collect 1M Dragon Essence.Kill Endermen in the End to drop Ender Armor, and then Salvage it at the Void Slate.Make sure you have at least Luck 5 and Looting 3 on your weapon.
You can get Luck 6 and Looting 4 from Einary during Jerry's Workshop.
If you have a Personal Deletor, it's worth deleting the Ender Pearls you drop as your inventory fills up extremely fast.

Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Easy (2/10)
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a shovel.
Enchant the shovel with Efficiency and dig sand in the Mushroom Desert until Mining 12
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines.

Recommended Minions​


Recommended Minion Slots: N/A ​

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3 for Pickobulus.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000).
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!

Guide by Tanya~
