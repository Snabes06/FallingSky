Diversity

Unlock 40 unique Collections.17 farming, 13 mining, 7 combat, 6 foraging, 10 fishing are easy/possible on bingo (53 total collections)None.

Clown Collab

Defeat Bonzo 5,000 times.Receiving a carry is the quickest way to level this but it is possible with good gear.Use dungeon armor drops and Super Cleaver on the Berserker class.

Mithril Collector

Reach 30,000 Mithril Collection.Purchase a pickaxe from Bubu (enchant it with Efficiency 5 and Fortune 3), and mine Mithril in the Dwarven Mines. Use your Pickobulus on large veins of blue wool Mithril.Unlock Pickobulus at HOTM 3, and use it as often as possible.

Emerald Treasure

Unlock an Emerald Dungeon Chest.Get a carry with 230 score, or do this with good gear and party finder.Use dungeon armor drops and Super Cleaver on the Berserker class. If you get an expensive chest, it might be worth to reroll it.

Pufferfish Collector

Reach 3,500 Pufferfish Collection.Fish until you can craft a Fishing Minion, and place them on private island. Use the fish from the Fishing Minion to craft the other 4 Fishing Minions.You could get Salmon armor from this for the Quick Reel goal.

Scavenger

Loot 250M Coins through the Scavenger EnchantmentKill Endermen in the End using Dreadlord Sword and wise Ender Armor, or with Void Sword enchanted with Scavenger 3 and fierce Ender Armor.Dreadlord Sword has Scavenger 5 by default

Spider Queen

Summon Arachne.Kill an Arachne Keepers for an 1 Arachne’s Calling, get someone else to help you spawn it. You could alternatively kill 4 Arachne Keeper’s to spawn it purely by yourself.Arachne Keepers have a 100% chance of getting an Arachne’s calling

Quick Reel

Summon 3 sea creatures within 75 seconds.Use Angler Armor (from Fisherman NPC in Hub), and the best rod you have enchanted with Angler 5.You could use Salmon Armor from Fishing Minions, however it is unnecessary.

Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: 4/10
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a sword.
Go to the Spider's Den and get the 3 easy relics (-142, 82, -340/-201, 83, -352/-341, 122, -252).
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines.

Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000)
Mine sand to reach mining XII for the Dwarven Mines.
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!
