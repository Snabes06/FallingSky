Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (6/10)

All Goals​

Row #1​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Pet

Gain 2.5B Pet experience.Passive.Use Simple Carrot Candies on different pets. Silverfish and Bee Pet are good picks, as they are easy to obtain in bulk.

Titanic Reforger

Apply the Titanic reforge to an item.Reforge any armor piece at the Hub Blacksmith.None

Warrior

Obtain level 18 in the Combat Skill.Kill Glacite Walkers in the Dwarven Mines until Combat 12 is obtained. Then go to the End to kill Endermen using the Void Sword and Ender Armor.Talk to Ike in the Spiders' Den to unlock Mob Types for some easy Combat EXP.

Talk to Bramass Beastslayer to unlock the Bestiary.

Ferocious

Reach 3 ? Ferocity.Apply the Dirty reforge using the Dirt Bottle obtained from the Smithmonger NPC in the Hub, and apply it to a Sword. Then do /visit ooffyy to eat Century Cakes which provides a buff of 2 Ferocity.An SOS flare can also complete this goal.

Skill Average

Reach a non-cosmetic Skill Average of 17.Self Explanatory.Foraging in Galatea gives really good EXP yields. Craft a Treecap, purchase Canopy Armor from Amuary, then chop fig trees. Make sure you've spoke to Agatha so you get bonus EXP and tickets for Frog Pet.
You can easily reach Fishing 10 by completing the End Race in under 80 seconds to obtain Nope the Fish, and then giving it to Carol in Galatea for 10,000 fishing EXP.
Brew Absorption potions with Enchanted Gold Ingots to level Alchemy.
Row #2​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Extra Storage

Craft a Large Storage.Go to Galatea and participate in Agatha's contest by mining Berry Bushes. This will give you free Forest Whispers which will be used to level up both Foraging Fortune and Sweep in the HOTF tree until you reach Level 20 in both.

Mine Oak Wood with an Efficiency 5 gold axe and haste.		None

A Hard Day's Work

Collect 100k Jacob's Tickets from Farming Events!Participate in Jacob's contests.None

Weird Flex

Gain 5 ? Strength from a Training Weight.Get a Training Weight from dungeons and wait 100 minutes with it in your inventory.You should try to do this as early as you can.

High Score!

Obtain a score of 230 or higher on Floor 1 of Catacombs.Obtain a Carry.None

Quick Reel

Summon 3 sea creatures within 75 seconds.Enchant a fishing rod with Lure 5, Piscary 5 and Angler 5. Equip Angler Armor from the Fish Merchant.

Fish anywhere until you reach this goal.		Minnow Bait can also be purchased from the Fish Merchant to increase Fishing Speed.
Spiders' Den Rain increases Fishing Speed also.
Row #3​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Acacia Log Collector

Reach 125,000 Acacia Log Collection.Craft and place eight T1 Acacia Minions.This will take about 4.5 days.

New Savings

Craft a Piggy Bank.Craft and place two T1 pig minions.This will take about 2 days.

Essence Of Spiders

Collect 1M Spider Essence.Kill Arachne in the Spiders' Den.Higher level bosses will drop more Essence.
Remember to salvage Arachnal gear!

Amber Crystal

Obtain an Amber Crystal in the Crystal Hollows.Reach Heart of the Mountain 4 by doing Dwarven Commissions.
Use 3 stacked Goblin Eggs of any variety on the king.		Find the King and Queen before getting the King's Scent.
You can use a Wishing Compass to help your search.
You can get goblin eggs from opening chests or killing Goblins in the Dwarven Mines.

Fashionable

Wear 4 unique Armor Sets.Passive.IMPORTANT:
Keep armor sets in wardrobe/ender chest, then wear when you have 4 sets. The goal is currently glitched, and progress resets when you switch lobbies.
Row #4​
Description​
Method​
Notes​

Petsitter

Upgrade a pet with Kat.Craft a Silverfish Pet and give it to Kat.Upgrade it to Rare, Epic or Legendary for extra Mining Wisdom.

I've got Chills!

Wear 4 pieces of the Glacite Armor Set.Kill Glacite Walkers in the Dwarven Mines.None

Collecting Data

Reach Bestiary Milestone 5.Passive.Make sure to talk to Bramass Beastslayer in the Spiders' Den

Agricultural Revolution

Harvest 10M crops.Farm Sugar Cane in the Garden.Agricultural Revolution counts blocks mined, not crops obtained. Fortune does not matter.

Pair Up!

Find 3 Pairs in the Experimentation Table.Reach Enchanting 10 and craft an Experimentation Table.Do as many experiments as you can, for Skill Average and Guardian Pet.

Description​
Method​
Notes​

Taking Flight

Consume a Magical Mushroom Soup.Use a Silk Touch axe to mine Mushroom Blocks in the Mushroom Desert to unlock the recipe.You can obtain Glowstone Dust by breaking Glowstone purchased from the Builder in the Hub.

Strongman

Reach 160 ? Strength.Hold a Void Sword while wearing at least 2/4 Ender Armor.None

Meiser

Store 50,000 Coins in your Bank.Passive.None

Pet Hoarder

Obtain 12 unique Pets.Craftable Pets (6)
Jerry, Pig, Chicken, Silverfish, Zombie, Horse
Non-Craftable Pets (6)
Bingo, Grandma Wolf, Bee, Enderman, Rock, Frog		NoneYou can replace any of these with Owl Pet (2.2m) or Reindeer (Jerry's Workshop).

If you're going for Fishing EXP, Lava Sea Creatures DO NOT count to Dolphin Milestone.

Frog Pet requires unlocking Agatha's Contests, which requires 1.21.5 and Galatea.

Goblin's Treasure

Kill 10k Golden Goblins.Mine Ores in the Dwarven Mines.Level up your Luck of the Cave and Efficient Miner perk.

Keep an eye out for 10x Goblin Chance during Skymall.

If there is a Mining Fiesta, it might be worth getting Mineral Armor for the extra Mining Spread.


Difficulty​

Overall Bingo Difficulty: Medium (6/10)
​

Step-by-Step for the Start​


Once bingo opens lobby hop until you can create a profile.
/hub immediately and go to the designated hub in splashes for a splash.
Equip your Bingo Pet and begin farming wheat, sell an inventory of wheat for quick coins and buy a shovel.
Enchant the shovel with Efficiency and dig sand in the Mushroom Desert until Mining 12
Unlock The End and Dwarven Mines
Reach Coal 3 (250 coal collection)
Purchase 640 Coal from the Mine Merchant
Purchase 640 Oak Logs from the Lumber Merchant
Chop 115 Oak Logs, then use the /trades menu to convert the 755 Oak Logs to 1510 Coal, for a total of 2400 coal.
Craft 15 Enchanted Coal, and give to Rhys to unlock Heart of the Mountain

Recommended Minions​


Recommended Minion Slots: 10
8 Acacia Minions
2 Pig Minions​

Additional Information


Tips/Notes:
Do your daily commissions until HOTM 3 for Pickobulus.
Remember to unlock the Mining Speed perk at HOTM 1.
Use Better Together (500 mining speed) and Gone With The Wind (300-600 mining speed) to your advantage.
You can get coins from doing relics in spider den. (Max 310,000).
Put #BingoBrewers after our splashes!
