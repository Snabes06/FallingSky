## Routing Notes (cross-tile synergies)
- Enderman combat cluster: `Lucky Looter`, `Scavenger`, `Enderman Slayer`, `Exterminator (mobs)`, `Strongman`, `Essence Of Dragons`.
- Dwarven mining cluster: `Powder Collector`, `Mithril/Gold/Diamond/Sand collectors`, `Rock Solid`, `Skilled (mining)`, `Goblin/egg goals`, `Commission*`.
- Fishing-minion cluster: `Pufferfish`, `Sponge`, `Prismarine`, `Tropical Fish`, plus `Quick Reel`/`Sea Summoner`/`Poseidon`/`King Of The... Seas?`.
- Garden cluster: `Dilmun Trader`, `At your service`, `A Hard Day's Work`, `Agricultural Revolution`, `Sugar Cane Collector`, `Exterminator (pests)`.
- Dungeon cluster: `Emerald Treasure`, `High Score!`, `Who needs friends?`, `A Lost Cause`, `KABOOM!`, `I See Stars`, `Weird Flex`.
